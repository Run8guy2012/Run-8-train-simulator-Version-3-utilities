Run8 Train Dispatch & Tonnage Utility

Standalone simulator-calibrated train makeup, HPT calculator, and DPU placement assistant.

About The Tool

This utility replaces manual guesswork in Run 8 train building. It calculates exact train length, gross tonnage, and horsepower-per-ton (HPT) ratios calibrated to Run 8's physical car tare weights and loaded specs.

Key Features

  • Quick Service Templates: Rapidly estimate intermodal, unit bulk, and manifest trains.
  • Custom Block Builder: Assemble cars block-by-block with equipment like 73ft centerbeams, Bethgons, and high-cube boxcars.
  • Smart Power Advisory: Calculates exact locomotive deficit and tells you how many units to add to hit target HPT.
  • DPU Cut Calculations: Recommends optimal DPU placement to protect drawbars and manage slack action.
  • Overpower Protection: Flags warnings when HPT exceeds safe thresholds to prevent wheel slip and draft force issues.
